Legislative Branch – Congress

Congress writes and passes the federal background-check law, making it mandatory for all gun sellers to be federally licensed; this includes sellers at gun shows and online sellers. All sales must have background checks through the NICS, even private sales. Congress will also release funds to help support the manpower and logistics of processing the background checks.

Executive Branch - President & Federal agencies

The executive branch will enforce the new law through its agencies: FBI, ATF. The FBI will administer all background checks and keep records through the NCIS. ATF will continue to monitor gun trafficking.

Judicial Branch – Courts

The courts interpret the Constitution and decide whether the gun law is legal and does not conflict with the Second Amendment. The judicial system will also impose harsher penalties for violations.
