Current Policy


The Brady Handgun Violence Prevention Act went into effect on February 28, 1994. This act created a nationwide system for background checks on firearm sales.

The requirement is that all federally licensed firearms dealers must process a background check on all potential buyers through the FBI, which led to the creation of the National Instant Criminal Background Check System (NICS).

“Like with any loophole or weak thread in a law, eventually over time nefarious actors are going to figure it out and it gets exploited”. -Jim Manley, a former Senate staffer to then-Majority Leader George Mitchell of Maine and Senator Edward Kennedy of Massachusetts

Current law allows for loopholes in certain firearms sales to take place without screening purchasers through NICS. Those loopholes are the Gunshow Loophole, private sales, and the Straw Purchase. This allows criminals to obtain guns for malicious intent.

In 2022, President Joe Biden signed the Bipartisan Safer Communities Act into law. This law strengthened federal gun regulations by making straw purchases and illegal firearm trafficking more clearly illegal under federal law.

The problem, though, is that every purchase needs a background check and this needs to be nationwide!
