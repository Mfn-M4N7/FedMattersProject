Challenges

  • Public fear of losing the Second Amendment right.
  • Having enough funds and manpower to complete background checks promptly. Background checks are instant but may take up to three days to respond. If it takes longer on the FBI’s part to return a status, by law the sale may go through. This sale is called a “default proceed”
  • Ensuring that local, state, and military report all records of violations to the FBI’s database. This includes mental health records by psychiatrists, dishonorable discharges, and domestic violence cases.
