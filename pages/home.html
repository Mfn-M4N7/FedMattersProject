Universal Background Checks


Gun violence prevention is an issue we must address, and universal background checks will aid in this. As both a parent and a student, school safety is something I think about often.

Federal gun policies affect the places where we learn, work, and raise our families. Gun violence impacts millions of people in the United States, including students, law enforcement officers, victims of domestic violence, and entire communities.

As the nation continues to debate gun rights and gun safety, the challenge remains finding policies that protect both constitutional freedoms and human lives. Supporters of universal background checks argue that requiring background checks for all firearms sales, including private sales and gun show transactions, helps prevent prohibited individuals from obtaining firearms through gaps in the current system. By closing these loopholes, advocates believe federal policy can create better public safety while still protecting the rights of law-abiding gun owners.
